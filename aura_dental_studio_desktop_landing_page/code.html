Believing oral health should never be accompanied by clinical dread, he founded Aura Dental Studio as a serene architectural haven that marries gentle robotic technologies with warm human empathy.

Dr. Vance completed rigorous fellowship training at the Academy of General Dentistry and maintains clinical research affiliations with top prosthodontic symposiums globally. He specializes in biomimetic tooth preservation, needle-free laser protocols, and full-arch restorative smile design.

Clarity On Your Studio Visit

Absolutely not. Our entire studio was intentionally engineered around fear-free biological care. By utilizing the Biolase® Waterlase system, we perform hard and soft tissue treatments using light photonic pulses and water mist rather than high-vibration friction drills. Additionally, we provide custom acoustic isolation, Bose noise-canceling headphones, ceiling display monitors, weighted blankets, and mild nitrous oxide sedation upon request.

We operate as an uncompromised fee-for-service studio and are happy to file out-of-network claims on your behalf for direct patient reimbursement. We also offer interest-free, 0% APR in-house payment memberships and flexible third-party medical financing through CareCredit and Proceed Finance.

We reserve daily emergency concierge slots specifically for chipped crowns, severe tooth pain, or sudden trauma. Existing and new patients can reach our priority triage line 24/7, and same-day treatment is accommodated within normal studio operating hours.

Biological dentistry respects oral wellness as fundamentally tied to your entire systemic immune health. We use only 100% biocompatible, metal-free, fluoride-free restorative compounds and test materials for biological compatibility. Laser techniques sterilize target areas instantaneously, accelerating post-treatment tissue recovery by over 300%.

Our studio is conveniently situated at 2100 Webster Street, Suite 400 in the heart of Pacific Heights. We offer complimentary subterranean valet concierge parking for all our registered patients during their appointments.
