## Brand & Style
This design system crafts a sanctuary of oral health that departs entirely from clinical apprehension. It bridges the precision of state-of-the-art diagnostic dentistry with the calm, enveloping comfort of a boutique boutique spa in San Francisco.

### Emotional Target
The interface evokes pristine reassurance, unhurried precision, and enlightened care. Interacting with the system should feel like stepping into a serene, light-filled architectural clinic overlooking the bay—refreshing, hygienic, reassuring, and distinctly premium.

### Design Movement: Warm Futuristic Minimalism
The aesthetic pairs clinical transparency with organic warmth:
- **Architectural Whitespace:** Massive, breathable layouts that signal hygiene and spacious clarity.
- **Glassmorphism & Luminous Layers:** Subtle frosted translucent panels that suggest optical lenses, clean water, and pure light.
- **Sculpted Geometry:** Soft, pill-inspired silhouettes (large corner radii) that eliminate sharp corners, conveying comfort and safety.

## Colors
The palette balances clinical sterility with organic calm and technological vigor:

- **Primary (`#2BB5B0` - Aqua Sage):** Evokes healing waters and fresh breath. Applied to key interactive motifs, icons, and focus states.
- **Secondary (`#0B1F3A` - Midnight Navy):** Provides high-contrast anchor weights, structured headings, and authoritative brand cues reflecting clinical expertise.
- **Tertiary (`#13D6CE` - Luminous Cyan):** Used sparingly for primary conversion actions, high-impact buttons, and subtle active glows.
- **Neutral Tint (`#F4FAF9` - Ice Mint Mist):** Soft, soothing foundational tone replacing harsh stark grays, eliminating eye fatigue.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) layered over subtle gradient bleeds of Ice Mint (`#E8F7F6`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Container):** Pure White with 90% opacity over frosted backdrops.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Overlays):** Translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with an integrated 1px border (`rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.15)`).
- **Text Primary:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1F3A`).
- **Text Secondary:** Slate Oceanic Navy (`#4A607A`).
- **Text Tertiary:** Soft Marine Grey (`#7E98A6`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm communicates high-fidelity medical expertise without traditional coldness:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs round, open geometric apertures that appear optimistic, friendly, and progressive. Tight letter tracking prevents float and feels intentional.
- **Body & Captions (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engineered for maximum reading comfort when patients review treatment plans, medical charts, and doctor qualifications.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Medium-to-bold weights with slight tracking expansion enhance scanning clarity across appointment slots and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a responsive 12-column fluid grid system with expansive exterior breathing room to emulate physical high-ceiling studio interiors.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns, 2rem gutters, 4rem canvas margins, max-content container bound to 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 2.5rem canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem canvas margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
Large sections transition with generous spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish an unhurried browsing pace. Card interiors prioritize top and bottom breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to prevent content compression.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics pristine physical layered elements—specimen glass, frosted partitions, and polished surfaces.

### Tonal Shadows & Light Bleeds
- **Atmospheric Glow:** Key surfaces cast tinted, ultra-soft teal-navy shadows instead of neutral black shades:
  - Default Elevated Surface: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.08);`
  - Floating Interaction: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -10px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.12), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.16);`
- **Glassmorphic Delicacy:** Layered elements use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with a soft ghost border: `1px solid rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.16)` on pure white backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
- **Z-Index Layering:**
  - Base: Flat canvas.
  - Surface: Treatment & testimonial cards with single-tier subtle elevation.
  - Modal/Drawer: Deep atmospheric frosted overlay with dual directional drop shadows.

## Shapes
Sharp angles are avoided across all interactive surfaces to alleviate surgical stress.

- **Primary Geometry:** Organic pill shapes and ultra-soft radii.
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** Fully pill-shaped or smoothed with large radiuses (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem).
- **Cards & Modals:** Radius range between 20px (`1.25rem`) and 28px (`1.75rem`), yielding an architectural, humanized enclosure.
- **Icons & Badges:** Framed inside soft circular and super-elliptical capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve Consultation"):** Luminous Cyan-Teal gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #13D6CE 0%, #2BB5B0 100%)`), midnight navy text (`#0B1F3A`), fully pill-rounded (`9999px`), with horizontal padding `2rem` and subtle teal under-glow.
- **Secondary / Studio Outlines:** Pure white surface, 1.5px border (`rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.15)`), midnight navy typography, with hover transition to faint mint wash (`#E8F7F6`).
- **Tertiary / Inline:** Navy text with subtle aqua underline animation on hover.

### Cards
- **Clinical Service Cards:** 24px border radius, white background, `1px solid rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.12)`, padded with `2rem`. Interactive states shift the border to `#2BB5B0` and lift the container with a soft dual shadow.
- **Practitioner Showcase:** Dual-layer layout featuring full-bleed curved photography overlaid with glassmorphic bio details docked at the bottom with 20px internal radius.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** 16px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, outline `1px solid #D6ECEB`. On focus: `2px solid #2BB5B0` with `0 0 0 4px rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.15)`. Text sits in Deep Navy with muted aqua placeholder labels.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Status & Specialization Chips:** Pill-shaped, background `rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.1)`, typography `#0B1F3A` in `label-sm`, with an optional mini status dot colored in vibrant accent `#13D6CE`.

### Lists & Timelines
- **Treatment Plans:** Connected timeline tracks featuring subtle teal lines (`2px solid rgba(43, 181, 176, 0.25)`) and circular step badges with soft glowing drop shadows.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Appointment Slot Selector:** Pill buttons displaying time and specialist availability; active state activates vibrant teal fill with pure white typography.
- **Smile Gallery Comparison:** Split-image slider framed inside a 24px rounded frosted panel with glass draggable controller handles.
